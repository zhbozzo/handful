# What we learned from giving apps already on the market

A short review (September 2026) of how established donation products handle the moments Handful cares about,
and what we took — or deliberately didn't take — from each.

| Product | Pattern | Evidence | In Handful |
|---|---|---|---|
| **GoFundMe** | Visible recent donations and a progress bar next to the ask | GoFundMe's own tests: removing the live activity feed cost 9.1% of conversions; removing the progress bar, 7.2% ([GoFundMe Pro](https://pro.gofundme.com/c/blog/nonprofit-social-proof-strategies/)) | Cause page shows the donor count, a small stack of recent donors and the last three gifts — **anonymous by default**; your own gift says “You”. |
| **GoFundMe** | Ask donors to share right after giving | One share brings about $13 more on average; shared fundraisers raise ~3× more ([GoFundMe](https://www.gofundme.com/c/crowdfunding-lessons/sharing)) | Share button on every cause, and “Ask a friend to help finish it · $X to go” on the success screen. The message names the need, never a person. |
| **GoFundMe** | Optional “tip” preselected at 14–16.5% | Widely criticised for surprising donors with a higher total ([Bloomerang](https://bloomerang.com/blog/gofundme-asks-donors-to-tip-them-whats-up-with-that), [GoFundMe Help](https://support.gofundme.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051144952-Choosing-a-tip-amount)) | **Not taken.** The gift sheet ends with “Total $5 · No platform fee · no tip, ever”. |
| **charity: water** | “100% model”: private donors fund operations so every public dollar reaches projects; donors see photos of the project they funded ([charity: water](https://www.charitywater.org/our-approach/100-percent-model)) | — | Handful Supporter (RevenueCat subscription) plays the same role: it pays for the platform so gifts carry no platform fee. Proof photos go through Privacy Shield first — no GPS, no faces. |
| **DonorsChoose** | Line-item budget; a “thank-you package” with a letter, photos and a report of how each dollar was spent ([Help Center](https://help.donorschoose.org/hc/en-us/articles/202137803-The-DonorsChoose-Thank-you-Package)) | — | Every cause is a priced item list; proof is receipt + note + protected photo, with leftovers moved to the next cause. |
| **ShareTheMeal (WFP)** | One-tap giving, amounts translated into something concrete (“$0.80 feeds a child for a day”), a notification when food is on its way ([WFP](https://innovation.wfp.org/project/sharethemeal), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sharethemeal-charity-donate/id977130010)) | — | “$5 covers the toiletry bags ×6”, one-tap “Complete it”, and a “Delivered ✓” notification. |
| **Kiva** | “Last-minute heroes” finish loans that are about to expire ([Kiva](https://www.kiva.org/lend-category-beta/ending-soon)) | — | “Almost there” carousel and “Complete it · $X” — urgency from the finish line, never from a countdown or guilt. |

## What we deliberately left out

- **Photos of people in need as covers.** Common on crowdfunding pages; Handful covers show the items being funded.
- **Countdown timers.** They raise urgency by pressure; the finish line (“$4 to go”) is enough.
- **Donor leaderboards / named donors by default.** Social proof without exposing anyone: counts and anonymous gifts.
- **Tips at checkout.** The platform is funded by the optional Supporter membership instead.
